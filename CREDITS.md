# Creator and credits

## Helder Nakaya — originator and creator

**VirtualTissue / Cellville3D was conceived and created by Helder Nakaya**, leader
of [CSBL — Computational Systems Biology Laboratory](https://csbiology.org) and
founder of [Hylix.app](https://hylix.app).

Helder originated the project and led its design, development and refinement. He
developed the entire project with **ChatGPT** and **Claude** as AI collaborators.
Credit for the project's conception and creation belongs to Helder Nakaya.

## AI development tools and cellular decisions

- **ChatGPT (OpenAI)** and **Claude (Anthropic)** supported Helder's development
  workflow, including implementation, review, documentation and iteration.
- **Jev from TypeSafe** supplies individual cellular choices in live experiments.
  Each cell receives only its local observations and actions permitted by the
  executable manual. The physical model, rather than the language model, executes
  the resulting effects. Bundled demonstrations use explicitly labeled fixtures.
- The GitHub hero artwork was generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation
  tool. It is a conceptual illustration, not a simulation result or microscopy
  image. Its prompt is preserved in [the artwork notes](docs/assets/README.md).

Scientific references and the distinction between supported mechanisms and
proposed numerical assumptions are documented in [Manual v3](manual_v3/Manual_v3.md).

Suggested attribution: **“VirtualTissue, conceived and created by Helder Nakaya
(CSBL; Hylix.app), developed with ChatGPT and Claude, using Jev from TypeSafe for
individual cellular decisions.”**
