# Experiment model

[Manual v3](../manual_v3/Manual_v3.md) is the scientific and operational reference.
Its machine-readable rules and supported-handler manifest are compiled into one
fingerprinted tissue pack consumed by the studio, recorder and server.

ETEC represents local adhesion and LT/ST exposure followed by a coarse,
Jev-selected epithelial ion/water response. EPEC has a distinct contact-dependent
attaching-and-effacing injury mechanism. IBD-like injury introduces a focal lesion
and impaired resolution; it is not a complete disease model. Baseline remains
quiet. Each scenario starts from a new run with cleared fields and ledgers.

Manual clocks, finite resources, local gates, exclusive targets and completion
receipts distinguish selection from executed biology. Unsupported pathways remain
blocked. See the manual for primary literature, proposed numerical assumptions,
cell identities, antigen handling, recruitment, death and differentiation limits.

Episodes preserve every successful individual decision, exact local context,
options/probabilities, event and transaction provenance, sampled visual states and
failed-request usage. A size, budget or upstream failure labels the recording
partial. No silent fallback or data truncation is permitted. Playback reads stored
data and never invokes a provider or advances biology. All distributed examples
are clearly labeled fixture demonstrations.
