Manual v3 · gut tissue rules

Every cell uses the same manual. Its identity, local inputs, contact, resources and ongoing processes determine its permitted actions. Jev chooses independently within that menu. Quiet cells maintain their baseline without an API call.

Induction takes 30–360 biological minutes; mucus release 1–15. Motility episodes take 1–10 minutes, antigen processing 1–12 hours, and monocyte adaptation 1–7 days under sustained permissive conditions. These are the manual’s uncalibrated duration priors. The progress bars show these clocks. Playback accelerates elapsed time, never shortens an action's duration.

Implemented host actions are listed above. Other manual actions remain blocked when their physical handler or required inputs are absent. They are never simulated as completed.

The tissue starts with the configured population. The default pack has 100 cells: 42 enterocytes, 8 goblet cells, 20 fibroblasts, 6 resident macrophages, 4 inflammatory monocytes, 2 dendritic cells, 10 neutrophils and 8 NK cells. Two monocytes and six neutrophils start in vascular reserve. The original six cell geometries are preserved; monocytes and dendritic cells have distinct morphology. All competence assignments are declared host assumptions.

ETEC colonization and LT/ST-induced ion transport, EPEC attachment, barrier injury and field transport use the existing proposed physical model. The IBD prompt is a focal innate inflammation challenge, not a complete disease model. Generic injury is not verified NK recognition.
